# Brand voice

- **Brand:** Cordillera Coffee Co. (fictional)
- **Three words for the tone:** warm, proud, simple
- **Spanish form of address:** tú
- **Words or phrases the brand likes:** "nuestra tierra", "taza de cada día"
- **Never say:** cheap, barato
- **Testimonials allowed?** no
- **Example of copy the client likes:** "Coffee from our mountains, for your everyday cup."
