## English
**Title:** Ají Dulce Hot Sauce – Mild, 5 fl oz | Sazón del Patio
**Meta description:** Mild ají dulce hot sauce made in Mayagüez, Puerto Rico. Flavor without much heat. Great on eggs, rice and beans, and grilled fish. 5 fl oz.
**Description:**
Flavor first, heat second. Sazón del Patio's Ají Dulce Hot Sauce is a mild sauce made with ají dulce peppers, vinegar, garlic, salt and cilantro, so you get real sabor de casa without much heat.

Made in Mayagüez, Puerto Rico, it's for anyone who wants a little kick of flavor on the table. Spoon it over eggs, rice and beans, or grilled fish. Shake well before using, and refrigerate after opening.

**Highlights:**

- Ingredients: ají dulce peppers, vinegar, garlic, salt, cilantro
- 5 fl oz bottle
- Made in Mayagüez, Puerto Rico
- Shake well; refrigerate after opening
